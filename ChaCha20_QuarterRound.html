Why ARX?

Addition mod 232 gives non-linearity through carry propagation.

Rotation shifts bits across registers without memory access.

XOR provides linear mixing over GF(2).

Rotations (16, 12, 8, 7)

  • 16: Half-word register swap
  • 12: Crosses 3 nibbles
  • 8: Full 1-byte lane shift
  • 7: Prime shift breaking alignments

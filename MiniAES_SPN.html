Why SPN? (Shannon's Principles)

1. Confusion (Substitution / S-Box): Hides the relationship between the key and the ciphertext using a highly non-linear lookup table.

2. Diffusion (Permutation / ShiftRow + MixColumn): Spreads single-nibble changes across the entire 16-bit block.
